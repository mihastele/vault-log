The story

We built vault-log for the people who have ever felt watched by their apps.

The best journaling tools should disappear into the background. They should let you capture a thought, a win, a regret, or a tiny bit of hope without asking you to trade your privacy for a calendar reminder.

We started with a simple idea: what if your journal felt like a real private space — one that keeps your words on your device, remembers what matters, and helps you look back without the pressure of public posting or algorithmic nudges?

“Not another app trying to monetize your inner life. A quiet place to keep it safe.”
Why people stay

Simple enough to use every day. Private enough to trust with your real thoughts.
